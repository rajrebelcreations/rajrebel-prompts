Girl Photographer Pose with DSLR Camera – AI Photo Prompt

Create a stylish, realistic portrait of a girl sitting casually on the floor and holding a DSLR camera up to one eye, just like a professional photographer. The outfit is an oversized cream button-up shirt with a white inner top, light-blue wide-leg jeans and white socks, with a relaxed and confident smile. The prompt uses the uploaded photo as the facial identity reference, so the result stays clearly recognisable as the same person. The output is a 9:16 vertical image, ready for Instagram stories, reels covers and phone wallpapers. It works well for anyone who loves photography, fashion or a clean aesthetic look, and suits a girl's photo or an AI girl character.

How to use

1. Tap "Copy" on the prompt below. 2. Open ChatGPT (or Gemini) and upload one clear photo of the girl, showing her face clearly. 3. Paste the prompt and send it. 4. Wait for the 9:16 image to generate. 5. If the pose or face is not right, ask ChatGPT to "keep the same face and match the pose exactly" and generate again. 6. Download the image and use it as a story, post or wallpaper.

Prompt

PROMPT
Create image Use the uploaded girl’s photo as the facial identity reference and keep her clearly recognizable. Create an ultra-realistic 9:16 vertical DSLR portrait in the exact style of the reference image.
Show her sitting casually on the floor in the same pose, one leg folded across the floor and the other knee raised outward. She holds a black DSLR/mirrorless camera vertically in front of one eye with one hand, while her other hand rests naturally near her folded leg. Give her a subtle, confident smile while looking toward the camera.
Dress her in the oversized loose off-white/cream button-up shirt, white inner top, relaxed light-blue wide-leg jeans, and simple white ankle socks. Keep the clothing natural, minimal and slightly relaxed.
Give her the hairstyle — long, thick dark-black hair, center/soft side parted, naturally loose with smooth voluminous waves and layered ends falling over both shoulders.
Use a minimal bright cream-white indoor studio, clean floor and large white window/blind background. Recreate the same soft natural daylight coming from the right side, producing creamy highlights, gentle shadows and an airy warm-neutral tone.
