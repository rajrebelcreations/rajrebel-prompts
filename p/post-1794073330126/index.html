Prompt

PROMPT
Vertical 9:16, 10-second cinematic Diwali night video photoshoot reel, photorealistic, 4K, NORMAL REAL-TIME SPEED – NO SLOW MOTION. Exactly 4 shots with hard cuts: shot 1 = 2 seconds, shot 2 = 2 seconds, shot 3 = 3 seconds, shot 4 = 3 seconds. All 4 shots must appear in this order. Every movement is smooth and continuous inside each shot, with no jumps, no skipped frames and no sudden pose changes.

FACE LOCK – HIGHEST PRIORITY: Using the provided reference images of the woman, she is exactly this same person in all 4 shots. Her face, facial features, skin tone and identity come only from the reference images – do not redesign, beautify, slim, age or alter her face, and do not generate a new face. After every hard cut she is the exact same person as in the reference images. Keep her face sharp and evenly lit with soft front light in every shot. Only the outfit, jewelry, hairstyle, location, lighting and actions are new.

[0-2s] SHOT 1 – SPARKLER LOOK-UP LAUGH
Camera: full-body shot, low angle, 50mm lens look, slight handheld movement, shallow depth of field.
Action: She stands holding a lit sparkler stretched out to her side in her right hand, her left hand lightly holding her langa. She lifts the sparkler up near her face, tilts her head back slightly, looks up and laughs happily, then looks back toward the camera with a smile.
Background: tall golden chichubuddi fountains erupting behind her on both sides.

[CUT]

[2-4s] SHOT 2 – CLOSE-UP LAUGH
Camera: close-up from chest up, eye level, 85mm lens look, very shallow depth of field.
Action: She holds one lit sparkler raised beside her face and laughs big and naturally with teeth showing, head slightly tilted, eyes crinkling with joy, then the laugh softens into a sweet smile at the camera.
Background: dark night with soft blurred golden bokeh and small fireworks.

[CUT]

[4-7s] SHOT 3 – NATURAL TWIRL (3 seconds, smooth)
Camera: full-body shot, eye level, steady camera, 35mm lens look.
Action: She opens her arms and does ONE smooth, natural, continuous full turn at a comfortable real speed, taking about 2 seconds for the full rotation. The rotation passes through every angle in order without skipping: front, her left side profile, back, her right side profile, and front again. Her langa gradually flares out into a wide circle as she turns, her voni and long hair swing outward and follow the motion naturally. She slows down and stops facing the camera, arms still open, and laughs. Her hair stays exactly the same at every angle.
Background: golden chichubuddi fountains spraying sparks behind her, fireworks in the sky.

[CUT]

[7-10s] SHOT 4 – SPARKLER FACE FRAME + CUTE ENDING (3 seconds)
Camera: medium close-up from waist up, front, eye level, 85mm lens look, very shallow depth of field.
Action: She holds a lit sparkler in each hand together in front of her chest. She slowly raises both sparklers up close to her face, one on each side of her face beside her cheeks, the sparkling tips just above her ears and the sticks slanting inward, so her face is framed in a V between the two glowing sparklers. She looks straight into the lens with a sweet smile, then tilts her head slightly to one side and gives a cute, playful smile with her nose slightly scrunched.
Background: dark night, soft golden bokeh and diya lights.

OUTFIT – identical in all 4 shots – traditional Telugu half saree (langa voni), elegant and modest:
- Langa: rich rani pink pure silk pattu langa with a broad shimmering gold zari border and small gold buttas.
- Blouse: matching rani pink silk blouse, elbow-length sleeves with gold zari embroidery, normal decent length.
- Voni: shimmering light-gold tissue silk voni with a fine zari border, wide enough to fully cover the waist and midriff from the front, the sides and the back at all times, including during the twirl. Draped from the waist across the chest and over the left shoulder.
- Jewelry: gold temple choker, long gold jhumkas, gold maang tikka, gold vaddanam over the voni, gold and pink glass bangles.
- Hair: long, straight, dark open hair with no flowers or hair accessories, looking the same from the front and the back, never covering her face.

LOCATION – same in all shots: Diwali night in the front yard of a modern Telugu home, background kept dark and softly blurred: rows of glowing clay diyas on the ground and a low wall, a colorful rangoli on the floor, tall golden chichubuddi fountains erupting, golden sparks and bokeh floating in the air.

LIGHTING & COLOR: Soft warm front fill light keeps her face and the pink-gold outfit bright, clear and richly colored; warm golden glow from the sparklers and fountains; golden rim light on her hair; deep dark night background. Warm cinematic Diwali color grade.

EXPRESSION: Happy, natural, lively and cute. Real laughs and smiles with teeth showing that come and go naturally, never frozen, never over-acting.

AUDIO: Crackling sparklers, whoosh of fountains, distant firecrackers. No dialogue, no music.

AVOID: jump or skipped frames during the twirl, sudden pose change, teleporting, new or different face, face change after cuts, face morphing, beautified or plastic face, flowers appearing in hair, hairstyle change, face hidden by hair or sparks, blurred face, distorted teeth, slow motion, skipping or merging shots, extra shots, exposed waist or midriff, outfit change between shots, extra fingers, deformed hands, more than two sparklers, dark underexposed face, daylight, Western clothes, text, subtitles, watermark, logos.
