PROMPT

Use the uploaded male close-up photo as the exclusive facial identity reference for the male character. The male character must look exactly like the person in the uploaded photos. Preserve his original face, youthful appearance, natural physique, body proportions, shoulder width, chest, waist, stomach, arms, and overall appearance. The close-up photo controls his face, and the full-body photo controls his body. Do not create a different-looking person.

Do not make the man look older, heavier, fatter, thinner, wider, bulkier, or more muscular than his uploaded full-body reference. Do not change his natural body shape or facial appearance. Preserve the woman's uploaded facial identity exactly. Never blend, replace, or redesign either person's identity.
