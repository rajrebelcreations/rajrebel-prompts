Romantic Rain Couple Video with Umbrella – Google Flow AI Video Prompt

Want to turn your and your partner's photos into a beautiful romantic rainy-day video? With this Google Flow AI video prompt, you can create a lovely couple scene featuring a man and woman standing together under a black umbrella in the rain. The video captures sweet romantic moments, gentle eye contact, natural expressions and a beautiful rainy atmosphere with glowing streetlights and cinematic lighting. Use two separate individual photos to create a personalized romantic video together. This AI video prompt is perfect for couples who want to make creative videos for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp Status. The prompt is designed to maintain both partners' original facial identities while creating realistic movements and romantic scenes.

How to use

1. Tap "Copy Prompt 01" and open Google Flow. 2. Upload your individual photo and your partner's individual photo as reference images. 3. Paste Video Prompt 01 and generate the first video clip. 4. Download the first clip and extract its last frame. 5. Start a new generation in Google Flow and upload the last frame of Clip 1 as a reference image. 6. Also upload both original individual photos and paste Video Prompt 02. 7. Generate the second clip and combine both clips to create your complete romantic rain couple video. Tip: Always add the last frame of Clip 1 along with both original photos when generating Clip 2 to maintain better face and scene consistency.

Prompts

PROMPT 1
Photorealistic cinematic short film scene, 9:16 vertical, 10 seconds, shot like a high-budget Indian romantic movie.

CHARACTERS (keep both faces exactly identical to the reference images):
- MAN: the man from the reference photo. He is of average height, about 5 feet 3 inches (160 cm), with a normal natural body build and realistic proportions. Formal look: crisp light sky-blue formal shirt, sleeves neatly folded to the forearm, shirt fully tucked into navy blue formal trousers, slim black leather belt, polished black formal shoes, silver wristwatch, neatly styled hair. He holds a large open black umbrella.
- WOMAN: the woman from the reference image. Soft peach chiffon saree with a thin golden border, elbow-length matching blouse, small gold jhumka earrings, small bindi, long loose black hair, slightly wet. She is about 5 feet 0 inches (152 cm), slightly shorter than the man, her head reaching his eye level.

LOCATION: night, heavy rain, a charming old bus stop with a curved tin roof on a quiet tree-lined Indian city street. A big gulmohar tree with red flowers, glowing vintage street lamps, soft mist, blurred warm shop lights, car headlights as soft bokeh, rain drops glowing in the light, puddles reflecting golden and blue lights. No readable signs, no logos.

ACTION (frame layout: the woman stands on the RIGHT side of the frame under the bus stop roof, the man comes from the LEFT side):
[0-3s] The woman stands under the roof, wet and shivering. The man walks in from the LEFT edge of the frame, walking forward naturally toward her, holding the open black umbrella. He stops in front of her, holds out the umbrella and says softly in Telugu: "ఇదిగోండి… మీరు తడిసిపోతున్నారు."
[3-6s] She takes the umbrella, surprised, and asks in Telugu: "మరి మీరు తడిసిపోరా?"
[6-10s] He smiles and says in Telugu: "పర్లేదు… నాకు వర్షం అంటే ఇష్టం." Then he slowly and naturally turns his body toward the camera, his face visible while turning, and walks forward out of the frame to the LEFT, the same direction he came from, into the rain. His feet step forward naturally and his body faces the direction he walks.

CAMERA & LOOK: static front-facing medium wide shot, both characters fully visible, camera at natural eye level, realistic human proportions, no exaggerated height, 35mm anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, natural skin texture, smooth realistic natural walking, no reverse walking, no body flipping, no sudden teleporting, accurate lip sync, rich cinematic color grading with warm golden lights and cool blue rain tones, film grain.

AUDIO: natural Telugu dialogue, heavy rain ambience, distant thunder, no background music. No text, no subtitles.
