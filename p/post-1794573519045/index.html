Create an ultra-realistic, premium romantic traditional Indian couple portrait in a beautiful ancient South Indian temple setting.

SCENE & BACKGROUND:
A stunning traditional stone temple with beautifully carved pillars, detailed ancient architecture and wide stone steps. Decorate the temple pillars with vibrant orange and yellow marigold flower garlands. Include traditional brass oil lamps, small brass flower bowls filled with white jasmine flowers and subtle festive decorations. Soft sunlight enters through the temple courtyard, creating a warm, peaceful and romantic atmosphere.

OUTFIT – MALE:
Wear a premium ivory-white traditional Indian kurta with a subtle linen texture, relaxed fit and long sleeves casually rolled up to the forearms. Pair it with a traditional white dhoti or veshti featuring a beautiful golden zari border. Add an elegant silver wristwatch. Keep the overall appearance clean, sophisticated and traditional.

OUTFIT – FEMALE:
Wear a beautiful traditional cream-white silk saree with a rich magenta-pink and golden zari border. Pair it with a matching magenta short-sleeved blouse featuring delicate golden embroidery. Add elegant traditional gold jhumka earrings, a delicate gold necklace, red and golden bangles and a small traditional bindi.

Style her long dark hair in a beautiful traditional hairstyle, decorated with fresh white jasmine flowers (Gajra) along the side and back of her hair.

POSE & BODY LANGUAGE:
Create a beautiful romantic couple moment on the ancient temple steps.

The woman sits gracefully in the foreground, slightly turned towards the camera. Rest her left cheek gently on her left palm, with her elbow supported on her raised knee. Give her a beautiful natural smile while looking slightly upwards towards the man.

The man sits closely beside and slightly behind her on the right side. Lean gently towards her with an affectionate expression.

He carefully places a small fresh white jasmine flower near her ear using his right hand. His other hand should rest naturally near her shoulder or beside her.

The woman looks lovingly towards him while he decorates her hair with the flower. Create a genuine, intimate and affectionate traditional couple moment.

Keep the hands, fingers and physical interaction anatomically correct and natural.

CAMERA & COMPOSITION:
Vertical 9:16 romantic traditional Indian couple photography. Medium three-quarter composition, capturing both subjects from head to below the knees. Position the woman in the foreground on the left and the man slightly behind her on the right.

Use a slightly elevated eye-level camera perspective to capture the temple steps, carved pillars and floral decorations. Keep both subjects clearly visible.

Professional 85mm portrait lens look with beautiful subject separation.

LIGHTING:
Beautiful warm natural morning sunlight entering from the temple courtyard. Soft golden highlights on their hair, faces, silk saree and traditional jewellery. Gentle shadows, balanced exposure and natural skin tones. Avoid excessive yellow tint.

COLOUR GRADING:
Elegant ivory white, vibrant magenta pink, antique gold, marigold orange and warm stone-brown tones. Premium traditional Indian wedding photography with a romantic cinematic atmosphere.

DEPTH OF FIELD:
Shallow depth of field, softly blurred temple architecture in the background, beautiful natural bokeh and sharp focus on both subjects. Keep the jasmine flowers and jewellery beautifully detailed.

QUALITY:
Ultra-photorealistic professional DSLR photography, 4K quality, realistic skin texture, intricate silk saree details, natural fabric folds, detailed temple carvings, realistic gold jewellery, anatomically correct hands with exactly five fingers and natural body proportions.
