AI COUPLE SLEEPING PHONE CALL — REALISTIC PHOTO PROMPT

Create an ultra-realistic, emotionally touching cinematic photograph of a young South Asian couple who have fallen asleep while staying connected on a late-night phone call. Use the uploaded reference photos as the absolute identity references for both individuals.

1. STRICT IDENTITY LOCK — HIGHEST PRIORITY

- Male: Preserve the exact face, facial structure, skin tone, hairstyle, hair texture, facial hair, and identity of the man in the uploaded reference photo.
- Female: Preserve the exact face, facial structure, skin tone, hairstyle, and identity of the woman in her uploaded reference photo.
- Do not blend their faces, change their identities, beautify them, or generate different people.
- Keep both individuals naturally realistic, with authentic skin texture and accurate facial proportions.

2. SCENE AND POSE
   A young South Asian couple is sleeping peacefully on the same bed, positioned in opposite directions with their heads close to each other. The man lies on the upper-right side of the composition, wearing a simple black long-sleeved sweatshirt. His eyes are closed, his head rests gently on his bent arm, and he holds a smartphone loosely in one hand near his face.

The woman lies on the lower-left side of the composition, facing toward the man, wearing a soft, light-colored floral pajama top with delicate pink flowers. Her long black hair is loosely tied in a messy bun, with a few natural strands framing her face. She has a tiny black bindi and subtle traditional earrings. Her eyes are closed, her expression is peaceful, and she holds a smartphone close to her face.

3. SMARTPHONE DETAILS
   Both smartphones display an active voice call screen with a dark interface. The caller name reads “My Love ❤️” on both screens, with realistic call controls and a red end-call button. Both phones must be clearly visible, naturally positioned in the couple's hands, with realistic screen reflections and accurate perspective. No extra phones or floating objects.

4. BEDROOM ENVIRONMENT
   Soft, light beige and pale-grey bedsheets, natural fabric folds, comfortable pillows, and a peaceful bedroom atmosphere. The couple is lying close together, creating a romantic long-distance-call-inspired mood, as if they fell asleep while talking to each other.

5. LIGHTING AND PHOTOGRAPHY
   Warm, soft bedside lighting mixed with gentle neutral ambient light. Natural skin tones, realistic shadows, sharp facial details, visible hair strands, authentic fabric textures, professional DSLR photography, 50mm lens look, shallow depth of field, high dynamic range, crisp focus on both faces and smartphones.

6. COMPOSITION
   Vertical 9:16 portrait composition. Overhead three-quarter camera angle, showing both faces, their upper bodies, hands, and both smartphones. The man occupies the upper half and the woman occupies the lower half. Maintain balanced spacing and a visually pleasing diagonal arrangement. Both faces must remain unobstructed and clearly recognizable.

7. FINAL QUALITY
   Photorealistic, intimate, natural, emotionally warm, premium photography, 4K-level detail, realistic anatomy, anatomically correct hands and fingers, accurate phone proportions.

NEGATIVE PROMPT: Different faces, identity changes, face swapping, altered skin tone, artificial beauty filters, cartoon, anime, CGI, plastic skin, exaggerated expressions, open eyes, distorted faces, deformed hands, extra fingers, missing fingers, duplicated phones, floating phones, unreadable phone interfaces, extra text, watermarks, logos, excessive orange lighting, harsh shadows, blurry faces, unnatural poses, unrealistic anatomy, collage layout, black borders.
