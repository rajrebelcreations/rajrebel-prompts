Romantic Couple Video from Photos – AI Video Prompt

Want to turn your favourite couple photo into a beautiful romantic video? With this AI couple video prompt, you can transform still photos into lovely animated moments featuring a couple walking together, enjoying a bike ride and sharing sweet romantic moments. The video style creates natural movements, attractive outdoor backgrounds and a warm cinematic atmosphere. This prompt is perfect for couples who want to create personalized AI videos for anniversaries, special occasions and romantic memories. You can use the generated clips for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp Status. When using your own reference photos, the prompt is designed to preserve both people's original faces and identities.

How to use

1. Tap "Copy Prompt" to copy the free AI couple video prompt. 2. Open Google Flow on your mobile or computer. 3. Upload your own individual photo as the first reference image. 4. Upload your partner's individual photo as the second reference image. 5. Paste the copied couple video prompt into Google Flow. 6. Generate the video and preview the romantic scenes. 7. Download your favourite result in the available quality. Tip: Upload clear individual photos showing both faces properly for better identity matching.

Identity, Body Proportions & Face Lock:
- Strict Identity & Body Source: The user-provided target photo is the absolute source for the exact facial identity, facial structure, skin tone, jawline, and slim body proportions. Do not alter, widen, or make the subjects look heavier/different; preserve their exact natural slim physique and personal appearance from the target photo. Do not use reference actors' faces or bodies.
- Chemistry & Expression: Natural, warm, deeply affectionate lovers' chemistry with glowing smiles, open-eyed eye contact, and close physical proximity.

Format & Style:
- Aspect Ratio: 9:16 Vertical
- Duration: 10 Seconds

Outfits & Styling:
- Male: Maroon casual shirt over a dark t-shirt, dark trousers (fitted slim cut).
- Female: Bright red kurti with a vibrant yellow dupatta and white leggings (fitted slim silhouette).

Environment & Backgrounds:
- Coastal seaside cliff with a rustic stone pillar, open tree-lined highway, and a lush green park in soft natural daylight.

Shot Breakdown & Continuous Action (00:00 - 00:10):
- 00:00 - 00:03: Medium Shot (Seaside Conversation). Couple stands close by a stone pillar on a coastal cliff. Female leans against the wall talking with a charming smile; male looks at her affectionately and smiles back.
- 00:03 - 00:06: Wide Tracking Shot (Romantic Bike Ride). Couple rides a blue motorcycle along an open road. Female sits pillion, hugging him tightly from behind with her arms around his waist as both laugh joyfully.
- 00:06 - 00:10: Medium Full Shot (Close Romantic Walk). The couple walks very close together on a park path; the male has his arm affectionately around the female's shoulder, holding her close as they talk, smile warmly, and look into each other's eyes with deep romantic chemistry.

Negative Constraints:
- Do not alter target face identity or body shape; no facial distortion, no unwanted weight gain/widening of body, no extra limbs, no lifting/spinning glitches, no text overlays, or watermarks.
