Cinematic Blue Neon Portrait AI Photo Prompt

Create a stunning cinematic portrait with blue neon lighting and a giant eye display. Preserve your original face and identity.

How to use

1. Open ChatGPT. 2. Upload your photo. 3. Copy and paste the prompt provided below. 4. Generate your image.

Prompt

PROMPT
/identitylock /posechange /cinematicportrait

REFERENCE IMAGE = ABSOLUTE IDENTITY SOURCE

Create a hyper-realistic cinematic portrait using the uploaded reference photo as the ONLY identity reference for the person.

1. STRICT IDENTITY PRESERVATION — HIGHEST PRIORITY

The person in the generated image must be the exact same person shown in the uploaded reference photo.

Preserve the original identity with maximum possible accuracy:

- Face: Exact original face, face shape, forehead, eyebrows, eyes, nose, lips, cheeks, jawline, chin, and facial proportions.
- Hairstyle: Preserve the exact hairstyle from the reference image, including hairline, hair length, texture, curls or straightness, volume, density, direction, and natural shape. Never invent a new hairstyle.
- Facial hair: Preserve the exact original moustache, beard, stubble, or clean-shaven appearance.
- Skin tone: Maintain the person's original natural skin tone and realistic skin texture.
- Body: Preserve the person's natural body build, proportions, shoulder width, and overall physical appearance.
- Age and gender presentation: Maintain the same appearance as the reference.
- Unique features: Preserve all distinctive facial features, marks, and other recognizable characteristics.

Do not beautify, redesign, reinterpret, replace, or regenerate the person's identity. Do not make the person look like a similar person. It must remain the same individual.

2. CHANGE ONLY THE POSE AND SCENE

Change the person's pose to a relaxed reclining position on a black metal folding chair in a dark, empty cinematic studio.

The person leans backward naturally, with the head tilted slightly upward, gazing toward a massive horizontal rectangular screen behind them.

The body posture, arm position, leg position, and viewing direction may change naturally to match the scene. Maintain realistic anatomy and the person's original body proportions.

Adapt the pose to the person's original physical appearance without altering their identity.

3. CLOTHING

Dress the person in a simple, elegant all-black outfit suitable for the cinematic scene.

Clothing may change to match the requested composition, but the person's face, hairstyle, facial hair, skin tone, and identity must remain unchanged.

4. GIANT SCREEN

Place a massive horizontal rectangular screen in the upper-middle background. Display an ultra-detailed close-up of a woman's expressive brown eyes, natural eyebrows, realistic eyelashes, and subtle strands of dark hair across her forehead.

The woman's eyes look directly toward the camera.

Surround the screen with intense electric cyan-blue neon edges and a bright white glow.

5. CINEMATIC LIGHTING

Use dramatic cyan-blue rim lighting from the screen to illuminate the person's hair outline, shoulders, and silhouette.

Maintain natural facial structure and skin tone under the blue lighting. Do not let lighting distort facial features or change the hairstyle.

Use deep navy-blue shadows, a dark empty studio, atmospheric volumetric glow, and a polished floor reflecting the cyan-blue light.

6. CAMERA AND COMPOSITION

Vertical 9:16 aspect ratio. Full-body composition. Place the person in the lower-center portion of the frame, seated on a black metal folding chair in a natural side-profile or three-quarter-profile pose.

The giant glowing screen dominates the upper-middle background, with generous negative space above.

Professional cinematic film still, realistic anatomy, lifelike skin texture, detailed hair strands, realistic fabric, high contrast, ultra-photorealistic, sharp details, high-resolution output.

7. STRICT NEGATIVE INSTRUCTIONS

No identity change, no different face, no generic AI face, no face swap, no altered facial proportions, no new hairstyle, no changed hairline, no different hair length, no altered curls, no changed moustache or beard, no skin whitening, no artificial beautification, no body reshaping, no age change, no gender change, no plastic skin, no cartoon appearance, no distorted anatomy, no extra fingers.

FINAL INSTRUCTION — NON-NEGOTIABLE

The uploaded reference photo determines the person's identity and appearance. The generated image must preserve the exact same face, hairstyle, hairline, facial hair, skin tone, and natural body characteristics of the reference person. Change only the pose, outfit, environment, camera angle, and lighting required by the scene. Never sacrifice identity accuracy for cinematic styling.

Apply this rule to every reference photo, regardless of who is in the image.
