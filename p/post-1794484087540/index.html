Stylish Café Portrait in a White Jacket – AI Photo Prompt

Prompt

PROMPT
Use my uploaded photo as the reference for the person. Keep my face exactly the same as my photo – same face shape, skin tone, eyes, hairstyle and moustache. Do not change my identity.

Create an ultra-realistic professional men's lifestyle portrait in a stylish modern café.

SCENE & BACKGROUND:
A cozy, premium café interior with warm beige walls, artistic abstract wall paintings, wooden furniture and orange café chairs. Include a few people sitting in the distant background, softly blurred to create a natural café atmosphere. Keep the background warm, elegant and minimal.

OUTFIT:
Wear a premium white sporty zip-up track jacket with two black stripes running along both sleeves, slightly open at the front, over a bright pink crew-neck T-shirt. Pair it with fitted black trousers. Keep the outfit neat, stylish and naturally fitted.

POSE & BODY LANGUAGE:
Sit casually on an orange café chair beside a wooden table. Rest your left elbow on the table and gently support your chin with your left hand, forming a relaxed fist. Keep your right hand resting naturally on your right thigh. Look slightly towards the right side of the frame with a calm, thoughtful and confident expression.

CAMERA & COMPOSITION:
Vertical medium-to-three-quarter portrait, eye-level camera angle. Capture the upper body, table, crossed visual lines of the arms and part of the seated legs. Keep the subject positioned slightly towards the centre. Use a professional 85mm portrait photography look with beautiful background separation.

LIGHTING:
Soft warm indoor ambient lighting, gentle natural light falling on the subject, balanced skin tones and subtle shadows. Avoid harsh flash and overexposure.

COLOUR GRADING:
Warm beige, white, black, vibrant pink and orange colour palette. Natural cinematic warmth with realistic skin texture and premium lifestyle photography aesthetics.

DEPTH OF FIELD:
Shallow depth of field, beautifully blurred café background, sharp focus on the subject, realistic bokeh.

QUALITY:
Ultra-photorealistic, professional DSLR photography, 4K quality, realistic clothing textures, anatomically correct hands, natural body proportions and authentic café environment.

IDENTITY LOCK:
Use my uploaded photo as the only identity reference. Do not copy the reference person's face or identity. Do not alter my facial structure, hairstyle or moustache. No face beautification, no artificial face replacement and no body distortion.

Vertical 9:16 (1080x1920). No extra text, no watermarks.
