AI Temple & Devotional Video Generation Tutorial | Google Flow & Gemini AI

Learn how to create cinematic devotional temple videos and stunning AI clips easily using Google Flow and Gemini AI. Step-by-step process with multi-clip generation and CapCut editing!

How to use

1. Open Google Flow or Gemini AI and upload your photo. 2. Paste the first prompt to generate Clip 1. 3. Take the ending frame of Clip 1 as a reference, upload your photo again, and paste the second prompt to generate Clip 2. 4. Join both clips in CapCut or any video editor to get your final cinematic devotional video ready!
