Romantic Garden Couple Video with Sweet Moments – Google Flow

Turn your and your partner's individual photos into a beautiful romantic couple video using Google Flow. This AI video idea features a couple meeting in a colourful garden, sharing a sweet smile, enjoying a gentle romantic moment and creating a lovely memory together. The video has a natural outdoor atmosphere with fresh greenery, blooming flowers, soft daylight and realistic movements. This Google Flow couple video prompt is perfect for couples who want to create romantic AI videos using their own photos. You can use the generated clips for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp Status. Upload both partners' photos as references to help preserve their original facial identities, outfits and appearance throughout the video.

How to use

1. Copy Video Prompt 01 and open Google Flow. 2. Upload your individual photo and your partner's individual photo as reference images. 3. Paste Video Prompt 01 and generate Clip 1. 4. Prompt

PROMPT
Create a highly photorealistic, natural-looking romantic Telugu montage in 9:16 portrait aspect ratio, with a total duration of 10 seconds.

STRICT FACE IDENTITY RULES
Use the uploaded male photo as the exclusive facial identity reference for the male character. Use the uploaded female photo as the exclusive facial identity reference for the female character. The faces must come ONLY from these two uploaded identity images.

The storyboard image is strictly a visual reference for camera angles, shot sizes, composition, poses, body language, outfits, backgrounds, lighting, scene order, and camera movements. NEVER copy, recreate, or use the faces of people appearing in the storyboard image. Do not substitute, blend, or mix identities. Preserve both uploaded identities, hairstyles, skin tones, and natural body proportions in every shot.

SHOT 1 — 0.0–1.5 SECONDS
Wide full-body shot of the man approaching the woman on a beautiful green garden walkway. He wears a simple white formal shirt neatly tucked into navy-blue formal trousers, a brown belt, polished brown formal shoes, and a simple wristwatch. The woman wears a pastel lavender embroidered salwar suit with a matching dupatta, small earrings, and her long dark hair styled naturally. She stands facing him. He approaches with a gentle smile. Bright natural morning light. Eye-level camera with a smooth slow tracking movement.

SHOT 2 — 1.5–3.0 SECONDS
Hard cut to a medium close-up of the woman in the same lavender embroidered salwar suit and matching dupatta. Her long dark hair falls naturally over her shoulders. She notices the man and turns her eyes toward him, giving a warm, genuine smile. Soft green garden background with natural daylight. Camera gently pushes in.

SHOT 3 — 3.0–4.0 SECONDS
Hard cut to a natural close-up two-shot of the couple in the same outfits and garden location.

The woman has one small loose strand of hair resting beside her cheek and ear.

The man gently raises his right hand and, with relaxed fingertips, softly brushes the loose strand away from her cheek and guides it behind her ear in one continuous, smooth movement.

His fingers lightly guide the strand into place. After tucking it behind her ear, he naturally lowers his hand away from her face.

The woman gives a subtle shy smile and slightly tilts her head toward him.

Keep the gesture very simple, delicate, and physically realistic. The movement should look smooth and natural from beginning to end. Soft garden light. Close-up camera, steady and intimate.

SHOT 4 — 4.0–6.0 SECONDS
Hard cut to a medium two-shot on a bright rooftop terrace. The man wears the same white formal shirt tucked into navy-blue trousers, brown belt, formal shoes, and wristwatch. The woman wears the same lavender embroidered salwar suit and matching dupatta. They stand facing each other and share affectionate eye contact with gentle natural smiles. Soft daylight and a distant city background. Eye-level camera with a subtle slow push-in.

SHOT 5 — 6.0–8.0 SECONDS
Hard cut to a medium shot on a garden bench. Both characters wear the same outfits. They sit close together. The woman gently rests her head on the man's shoulder, and he gives a peaceful affectionate smile. Natural greenery and flowers in the background. Soft bright daylight. Camera slowly moves closer.

SHOT 6 — 8.0–10.0 SECONDS
Hard cut to a close-up romantic ending in the same garden. The man gently gives the woman a brief cheek kiss. She closes her eyes and smiles shyly. Their expressions remain natural and subtle. Warm natural sunlight. Close-up camera with a gentle steady push-in. End on their affectionate expressions.

REALISM AND CONTINUITY
The output must look like genuine live-action footage captured with a professional cinema camera, not artificial or obviously AI-generated footage.

Every shot must look like a genuine candid moment captured in real life. All facial expressions, eye contact, smiles, head movements, hand gestures, body movements, and romantic interactions must be subtle, spontaneous, smooth, and physically natural.

Avoid stiff poses, robotic gestures, exaggerated reactions, artificial smiles, awkward pauses, sudden movements, or unnatural hand positions. Each action must flow naturally from beginning to end with realistic timing and believable reactions.

Use authentic facial expressions, subtle blinking, natural eye movements, realistic skin pores, individual hair strands, natural hand movements, physically accurate clothing, and spontaneous emotional reactions.

Preserve both uploaded characters' faces and identities throughout every shot. Follow the storyboard for composition, poses, outfits, camera work, lighting, and background, but NEVER use its faces.

Keep the male outfit exactly the same in every shot: simple white formal shirt tucked into navy-blue formal trousers, brown belt, polished brown formal shoes, and a simple wristwatch. No coat, blazer, suit jacket, or tie.

Keep the female outfit exactly the same in every shot: pastel lavender embroidered salwar suit with matching dupatta and small earrings.

The hair-tucking action in Shot 3 must be one smooth, gentle brush-and-tuck movement. The man's hand should move naturally and smoothly as he guides the loose strand behind her ear.

Use realistic depth of field, natural shadows, subtle cinematic color grading, and smooth camera movement. No face morphing, identity blending, body shape change, extra fingers, distorted hands, flickering, random background changes, unnecessary objects, text, captions, logos, or watermarks.

No dialogue, no lip-sync, and no music. No artificial transitions between scenes. Maintain the specified shot order and timing. Output in 9:16 portrait format.
