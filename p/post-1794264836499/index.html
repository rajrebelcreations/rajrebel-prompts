PROMPT
Use my uploaded personal photo as the ONLY identity reference. Create an ultra-realistic, premium men's fashion lifestyle portrait inspired by the reference image's composition, outfit, relaxed standing pose, bright indoor setting, natural daylight and photography style.

1. STRICT FACE & IDENTITY LOCK

- Preserve my exact original face and identity from my uploaded personal photo.
- Maintain my original facial structure, skin tone, eyes, nose, lips, jawline and all natural facial details.
- Keep my original hairstyle exactly as shown in my personal photo.
- Do not beautify, reshape, smooth or modify my face.
- Do not copy the face or identity of the person in the style reference.
- My personal photo must remain the only source of my identity.

2. OUTFIT & STYLING

- Wear a premium white linen or cotton casual kurta-style shirt.
- Minimal mandarin-inspired open neckline with a natural V-shaped opening.
- Relaxed, slightly loose-fitting shirt with elegant fabric texture.
- Sleeves folded naturally to approximately elbow length.
- Lightly tucked or naturally fitted around the waist.
- Wear premium dark beige or chocolate-brown tailored trousers.
- A stylish black wristwatch with a round dark dial on the left wrist.
- No additional accessories except the wristwatch.
- Maintain realistic fabric folds and natural clothing proportions.

3. POSE & BODY POSITION

- Standing casually beside a bright indoor window.
- Upper body slightly angled while facing the camera.
- Both forearms positioned naturally in front of the waist.
- One hand gently holding or resting over the opposite wrist.
- Relaxed fingers with anatomically correct hand positioning.
- Shoulders naturally relaxed.
- Comfortable, confident and sophisticated posture.

4. LOCATION & BACKGROUND

- Simple, elegant and uncluttered background.
- Subtle architectural wall details.

- Background softly blurred while maintaining a realistic indoor atmosphere.

5. LIGHTING & COLOR GRADING

- Beautiful soft natural window daylight.

- Subject positioned centrally with comfortable headroom.
- Camera positioned at natural eye level.
- Straight-on portrait perspective.
- Natural 50mm or 85mm DSLR portrait lens appearance.
- Aperture approximately f/2.8.
- Sharp focus on the subject with subtle background separation.
- Professional fashion editorial photography composition.

7. FINAL IMAGE QUALITY

- Ultra-photorealistic professional men's lifestyle photography.
- High-resolution 4K-quality appearance.
- Realistic skin texture and natural details.
- Detailed fabric texture and accurate clothing folds.
- Realistic hands, fingers and body proportions.

STRICT NEGATIVE INSTRUCTIONS

Do not change my original face, identity, hairstyle or skin tone. Do not copy the reference person's face. No extra people, no extra fingers, no distorted hands, no artificial skin, no excessive retouching, no cartoon effects, no dramatic cinematic lighting, no dark background, no text, no logos and no watermark.

FINAL GOAL: Recreate the reference image's overall white-shirt fashion style, crossed-wrist standing pose, beige-brown trousers, black wristwatch, bright window-side environment and soft natural daylight, while keeping my exact real face and identity from my uploaded personal photo unchanged.
