Create AI Couple Videos with Google Flow & Gemini AI

Create romantic AI couple videos using your partner's photo with Google Flow or Gemini AI.

How to use

1. Open Google Flow or Gemini AI and select your avatar. 2. Upload your partner's photo and paste Prompt 1. Generate the first clip. 3. Repeat with Prompt 2 to generate the second clip. 4. Join both clips in CapCut or any video editing app. Your video is ready!
