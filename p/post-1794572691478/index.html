Create an ultra-realistic, premium romantic Indian couple portrait in a beautiful outdoor garden during golden hour.

SCENE & BACKGROUND:
A lush green garden with mature trees, beautiful green foliage and a peaceful natural pathway. Warm golden sunlight streams through the trees from the upper-right background, creating a dreamy sunset atmosphere. Add soft golden bokeh lights and blurred colourful flowers in the distance.

OUTFIT – MALE:
Wear a premium dark brown-black checkered casual button-down shirt with a subtle small-check pattern, long sleeves casually rolled up to the elbows and a slightly open collar. Pair it with fitted black trousers. Add a stylish black wristwatch.

OUTFIT – FEMALE:
Wear a beautiful deep emerald-green traditional Anarkali-style outfit with elegant golden diamond-shaped lattice embroidery throughout the fabric. Include delicate golden detailing on the sleeves and neckline. Add matching traditional gold earrings, a delicate bracelet and a small elegant bindi. Style her long dark hair in soft, flowing waves.

POSE & BODY LANGUAGE:
Create a romantic standing couple portrait.

The man stands on the left side, facing slightly towards the camera with a confident, relaxed expression. Keep one hand naturally inside his trouser pocket.

The woman stands very close beside him on the right, slightly leaning towards his chest. Place her right hand gently on the man's upper chest, with her fingers naturally spread over his shirt. Her other arm rests naturally near his waist.

The man gently wraps his other arm around her waist, holding her close.

Both subjects should look towards the camera with natural, affectionate expressions. Maintain comfortable body contact and realistic romantic chemistry.

CAMERA & COMPOSITION:
Vertical 9:16 premium couple portrait. Medium three-quarter composition, capturing both subjects from head to upper thighs. Position the man on the left and woman on the right. Keep both faces clearly visible.

Eye-level camera angle with a professional 85mm portrait lens look. Natural body proportions and elegant framing.

LIGHTING:
Beautiful warm golden-hour backlighting from behind the couple. Soft golden rim light around their hair and shoulders. Gentle facial illumination, natural skin tones and balanced exposure.

COLOUR GRADING:
Rich emerald green, dark brown-black, warm golden sunlight and natural garden green. Premium cinematic romantic photography with luxurious colour tones.

DEPTH OF FIELD:
Shallow depth of field, creamy golden bokeh, softly blurred garden background and sharp focus on both subjects.

QUALITY:
Ultra-photorealistic, professional DSLR photography, 4K quality, realistic skin texture, detailed fabric patterns, natural hair texture, anatomically correct bodies, realistic hands with exactly five fingers and natural physical interaction.
