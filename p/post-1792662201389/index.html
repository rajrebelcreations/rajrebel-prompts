Durga Maa Rescue - AI Video Prompt

Create an emotional cinematic AI video where Goddess Durga rescues a falling man.

How to use

Open Google Flow or any AI video tool and select Image to Video mode. ​Upload your photo first, then upload the Goddess Durga image. ​Copy the prompt below, paste it into the tool, and generate your video clip.

Prompt

PROMPT
Create a 10-second ultra-photorealistic cinematic Indian devotional rescue video in vertical 9:16 at 24fps. Start immediately with action. No slow introduction.

IMPORTANT:
Keep the original concept, environment, waterfall, mountain geography, male devotee, falling physics, cinematic realism and overall visual style exactly the same.

The ONLY major character replacement is:
NO Lord Ganesha anywhere in the video.
Replace Ganesha completely with Goddess Durga seated majestically on a powerful realistic lion.

REFERENCE IMAGE 1:
Use Reference Image 1 as the absolute identity reference for the male devotee.

FACE IDENTITY LOCK — HIGHEST PRIORITY:
Preserve the exact face, facial structure, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, jawline, cheeks, forehead, ears, skin tone, hairstyle and facial hair from Reference Image 1.

The male devotee must remain the exact same person throughout all 10 seconds.
No face morphing.
No face drift.
No beautification.
No face replacement.
No age change.
No hairstyle change.
No body-shape change.
No generic AI face.

COSTUME LOCK:
Keep the devotee's clothing exactly consistent throughout the entire video. Do not change clothing, colors or accessories between shots.

GODDESS DURGA:
Goddess Durga is seated on a majestic, powerful, realistic lion integrated into the enormous mountain shrine.

She has a serene, compassionate and protective motherly expression.
She wears a traditional bright red silk saree with elegant gold borders, a golden crown and traditional gold jewelry.

Keep Durga photorealistic, majestic and devotional.
No cartoon appearance.
No plastic appearance.
No fantasy-game appearance.

THE ENTIRE 10-SECOND SEQUENCE:

0.0-2.5 SECONDS — CLIMB AND SLIP:
Immediately show the male devotee climbing the wet mountain ledge beside the huge waterfall.
Use a fast cinematic side/wide shot followed by a close shot of his boot slipping on wet moss.
Small rocks fall.
His hand loses grip.
He suddenly falls backward into open air.

2.5-5.5 SECONDS — FALL:
Show one continuous wide cinematic shot of the devotee falling naturally through mist beside the waterfall.

He must remain outside the main waterfall column.
Realistic gravity.
Natural body movement.
No floating.
No repeated spinning.
His clothes react naturally to wind and falling motion.

As he falls, reveal the enormous Goddess Durga seated on her lion in the mountain behind the waterfall.

5.5-8.5 SECONDS — DURGA RESCUE:
Extreme cinematic shot of Goddess Durga noticing the falling devotee.

She immediately reaches one hand toward him.

The devotee reaches upward and firmly grabs Goddess Durga's hand.

IMPORTANT RESCUE ACTION:
Goddess Durga personally catches his hand and pulls him upward.

NO TRUNK.
NO ELEPHANT.
NO GANESHA.
NO Ganesha-like rescue mechanism.

Her hand must physically connect with his hand.
She slows his fall and pulls him upward with believable strength and natural momentum.

No teleportation.
No instant snapping.
No stretched or rubber-like arms.
No impossible anatomy.

8.5-10.0 SECONDS — SAFE LANDING AND BLESSING:
Durga safely pulls the devotee onto the wet stone platform beside the lion.

His boots touch the platform.
He regains balance and immediately joins his hands in namaste while looking toward Durga with gratitude.

Durga remains seated on the lion and raises one hand in a calm blessing gesture.

Finish with a fast but smooth cinematic pull-back showing:
the devotee,
Goddess Durga,
the majestic lion,
the enormous mountain,
the waterfall,
mist and rainforest.

CAMERA:
High-budget live-action Indian devotional cinema.
Full-frame cinema-camera realism.
Natural motion blur.
Realistic depth of field.
Subtle lens breathing.
Realistic wet surfaces and atmospheric mist.
Fast cinematic pacing while keeping every action physically understandable.

ENVIRONMENT:
Keep the same rainforest waterfall environment throughout.
Wet dark mountain rocks.
Moss and lichen.
Heavy waterfall mist.
Natural overcast daylight transitioning into subtle warm sunlight.
REALISM:
Photorealistic human face.
Accurate Indian skin tone.
Natural skin texture.
Realistic wet hair and clothing.
Realistic gravity and momentum.
Physically believable hand-to-hand rescue.

ABSOLUTELY NO:
Lord Ganesha, elephant, elephant trunk, Ganesha statue, Ganesha face, Ganesha hand, Ganesha symbols, face drift, face replacement, beauty filter, changed identity, changed hairstyle, changed body shape, duplicate person, extra people, extra limbs, malformed hands, floating, teleportation, repeated spinning, unrealistic falling, rubber arms, stretched anatomy, cartoon Durga, plastic Durga, cartoon lion, malformed lion, glowing eyes, neon eyes, magical particles, energy waves, lightning, fire, blood, injury, horror, text, subtitles, logos or watermarks.
