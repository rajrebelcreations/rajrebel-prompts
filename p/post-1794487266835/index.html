PROMPT
Use my uploaded photo as the reference for the person. Keep my face exactly the same as my photo – same face shape, skin tone, eyes, hairstyle and moustache. Do not change my identity.

Create an ultra-realistic, premium romantic Indian couple portrait in a beautiful outdoor garden.

SCENE & BACKGROUND:
A lush green tropical garden with tall bamboo plants, fresh green leaves and elegant white architectural pillars. Create a bright, peaceful outdoor atmosphere with natural greenery filling the background. Add soft sunlight filtering through the leaves and beautiful natural background blur.

OUTFIT – MALE:
Wear a premium dark navy-blue casual button-down shirt with a slightly open collar and long sleeves. Keep the shirt neatly fitted with a sophisticated, minimal appearance. Add a classy black wristwatch.

OUTFIT – FEMALE:
Wear a beautiful royal navy-blue traditional saree with intricate silver-white floral and paisley patterns throughout the fabric. Pair it with a simple elegant white sleeveless blouse. Add traditional silver jhumka earrings and a delicate bracelet. Let the saree drape naturally over her shoulder.

POSE & BODY LANGUAGE:
Create a beautiful romantic couple pose.

The woman stands slightly in front of the man, facing the camera with a natural happy smile.

The man stands directly behind her, slightly leaning his head towards hers. Gently wrap both arms around her waist in a comfortable romantic embrace.

The woman's arms should rest naturally in front of her body, with one hand gently placed over the man's arm.

Both should have warm, genuine smiles while looking directly towards the camera. Keep their heads close together to create a loving and affectionate couple moment.

CAMERA & COMPOSITION:
Vertical 9:16 romantic couple portrait. Medium three-quarter composition, showing both subjects from head to below the waist. The woman is positioned slightly towards the left foreground, while the man stands behind her on the right.

Eye-level camera angle with a professional 85mm portrait lens look. Keep both faces clearly visible and the couple centrally framed.

LIGHTING:
Bright, soft natural daylight with gentle highlights on their hair and clothing. Balanced white lighting, natural skin tones and soft shadows. Avoid dark cinematic lighting and yellow colour casts.

COLOUR GRADING:
Rich navy blue, royal blue, silver-white, fresh green and clean neutral tones. Premium Indian fashion photography with a bright, natural and elegant aesthetic.

DEPTH OF FIELD:
Shallow depth of field, beautifully blurred tropical greenery, soft natural bokeh and sharp focus on both subjects.

QUALITY:
Ultra-photorealistic, professional DSLR couple photography, 4K quality, realistic skin texture, detailed saree embroidery, natural fabric folds, anatomically correct bodies, realistic hands with exactly five fingers and natural body proportions.

IDENTITY LOCK:
Use my uploaded personal photo as the ONLY identity reference for the man. Preserve my exact face, hairstyle, moustache, skin tone and identity. Do not copy the reference man's face.

Use the separately uploaded female photo as the ONLY identity reference for the woman. Preserve her original face, skin tone and identity. Never mix or swap faces.

Do not change either person's identity, do not beautify or modify their facial features, and do not add extra people.
