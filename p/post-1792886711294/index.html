Rajuni Kannave Sammakka - Emotional Cinematic AI Video Prompt & Tutorial

Learn how to create this highly emotional, cinematic Telugu historical-style AI video featuring a mother's sentiment, tiger element, and mass army setup using Google Flow and Gemini AI.

How to use

1️⃣ Google Flowలో ముందుగా Mother reference photo మాత్రమే upload చేసి, First Clip promptతో 4 seconds opening clip generate చేయండి. 2️⃣ తర్వాత Son + Mother రెండు reference photos upload చేసి, Main Clip promptతో 10 seconds second clip generate చేయండి. 3️⃣ రెండు clipsని CapCutలోకి తీసుకుని Original Song add చేసి, lyricsకి తగ్గట్టు Split & Sync చేయండి. అవసరమైన చోట Slow Motion, Cuts, Transitions add చేసి cinematicగా edit చేసుకోండి

Prompts

PROMPT 1
Create a 5-second cinematic historical Telugu movie opening shot, vertical 9:16.

Use the uploaded image as the EXACT visual reference for the mother, newborn baby, hut, forest, composition, costume and atmosphere.

The scene must look almost exactly like the reference image.

The mother is INSIDE the same small old mud-and-thatch hut at the beginning.

She is already holding her newborn baby securely in both arms.

She slowly walks from INSIDE THE HUT toward the doorway.

The camera is positioned outside the hut and watches her approach the doorway.

She then physically STEPS OUT THROUGH THE HUT DOORWAY while continuously holding the same newborn baby.

She takes two or three natural steps outside and looks lovingly down at the baby.

Keep the same hut, same large trees, same earthen ground, same rural forest surroundings and same warm early-morning sunlight as the reference image.

The baby must remain continuously visible in her arms from the beginning to the end.

IMPORTANT:
The mother must START INSIDE the hut.
She must WALK OUT OF THE HUT.
Show the complete movement:
INSIDE HUT → DOORWAY → OUTSIDE HUT.

Do not suddenly place the mother outside.
Do not make the baby appear suddenly.
Do not change the hut or environment.
Do not change the mother's appearance or saree.
Do not add any other characters.

Natural Telugu historical movie cinematography, realistic human movement, gentle backward camera tracking, sunlight filtering through trees, soft morning haze, cinematic depth, photorealistic 4K detail.

No text.
No subtitles.
No dialogue.
No modern objects.
PROMPT 2
Create a high-budget Telugu historical movie song sequence, vertical 9:16, exactly 10 seconds.

Use the uploaded SON reference image and MOTHER reference image as two separate character references. Preserve their identities exactly. The son must remain naturally lean, 5'2", 56 kg. Do not make him tall or bulky. Maintain the same costumes and historical world established in Clip 1.

The visuals must feel like REAL CINEMA — not posed portraits, not a slideshow, not literal illustrations of lyrics. Every lyric must trigger a new cinematic scene, with different locations, actions, compositions, camera angles and transitions.

LYRICS:

“కండ్లల్లో కన్నీళ్లు సమ్మక్క”

0–2 SEC:
Night after a long journey.
The mother sits alone inside a dark mud hut beside a tiny oil lamp.
The door is half open and moonlight enters through it.
She hears distant footsteps.
She slowly looks toward the doorway.
CUT to an extreme close-up of her eyes.
Her eyes fill with tears before she even sees her son.
Slow push-in.
The emotion is pride, fear and love together.

“నీ ఆశయం సాధించే సమ్మక్క”

2–4 SEC:
HARD CUT — completely different scene.
Early morning on a high rocky hill.
Mother stands behind her son.
She ties a traditional red cloth firmly around his wrist before he leaves for his mission.
She looks at him with determination.
He turns toward the vast forest.
Camera moves from the mother's hand → his eyes → the enormous landscape ahead.
Sun rises behind them.
The visual says: her dream is now his mission.

“ఇంకిందే రక్తము సమ్మక్క”

4–6 SEC:
HARD CUT — completely different scene.
A harsh battlefield landscape after a struggle.
Dust fills the air.
The warrior walks alone through abandoned weapons and broken wooden shields.
His clothes are dusty and worn.
There is only a tiny scratch on his hand.
He wipes the dirt from it and continues walking without stopping.
Low-angle tracking shot following his feet.
Then close-up of his determined face.
Do not show graphic blood.
Make the scene communicate sacrifice and the price of his struggle.

“వాడు పాములా పడిగిప్పే సమ్మక్క”

6–8 SEC:
HARD CUT — completely different scene.
Dense forest at night.
Enemies carrying fire torches search between trees.
The warrior is completely still in the darkness.
Camera moves extremely slowly behind tree branches.
Suddenly he slips from one shadow to another with extremely fast, silent movement.
One enemy turns — nothing is there.
The warrior appears behind another tree.
Quick whip-pan.
He disappears again.
The camera movement must feel unpredictable and snake-like.
Do NOT show an actual snake.
This is a visual metaphor for his dangerous agility.

“కదిలేటి సంద్రం వాడు”

8–10 SEC:
HARD CUT — massive cinematic climax.
Golden-orange sunset over an enormous valley.
The warrior stands alone on a high rocky cliff.
Camera begins with an extreme close-up of his eyes.
CUT to his feet stepping forward.
CUT to a low-angle silhouette.
Then the camera rapidly CRANES UP and PULLS FAR BACK.

Reveal a gigantic moving mass of villagers and warriors filling the valley behind him.
Dust rises from thousands of footsteps.
Traditional flags move in the wind.
The entire crowd advances together.

The warrior is small in the frame, but he is at the front of the moving human wave.

The final visual must feel like:
ONE MAN → BECOMES A MOVING SEA OF PEOPLE.

End with an enormous aerial wide shot of the sunset, warrior, crowd, dust and mountains.

CINEMATIC STYLE:
Telugu feature-film cinematography.
Real locations.
Realistic period production design.
Natural human performances.
Anamorphic widescreen feeling adapted to 9:16.
35mm wide shots.
50mm character shots.
85mm emotional close-ups.
Low-angle hero frames.
Ground-level tracking.
Whip-pan transitions.
Slow push-ins.
Crane shots.
Large aerial reveal.
Golden-hour backlight.
Moonlight and firelight contrast.
Atmospheric smoke and dust.
Strong foreground and background depth.
Film-quality motion blur.
Natural skin texture.
Rich cinematic lighting.

IMPORTANT:
Do not make every shot look beautiful just for beauty.
Each shot must visually tell the lyric.
Every lyric must have a NEW SCENE.
Every scene must move the story forward.
No static posing.
No random smiling.
No generic village shots.
No literal blood.
No literal snake.
No text.
No subtitles.
No dialogue.
No modern objects.
No face morphing.
No body transformation.
No costume changes.
