How to Create a Bava Maradalu Romantic AI Couple Reel

Want to create a romantic Bava-Maradalu AI couple reel using just two photos? In this tutorial, learn how to turn two separate individual photos into a beautiful romantic couple scene using ChatGPT and Google Flow. You can create a cute tea-time moment, a warm hug scene and other romantic moments with natural expressions and realistic movements. The process is simple and divided into three steps. First, upload both individual photos to ChatGPT and generate a Start Image. Next, use Google Flow to create the first 10-second video clip. Finally, continue from the first clip to generate the second 10-second clip. Create your own personalized AI couple reel for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp Status.

How to use

1. Open ChatGPT and upload your individual photo and your partner's individual photo. 2. Copy the Image Prompt and generate a romantic couple Start Image. 3. Open Google Flow and upload the generated Start Image. 4. Paste the Clip 1 Prompt and generate the first 10-second video. 5. Continue from Clip 1 in Google Flow using the Clip 2 Prompt. 6. Generate the second 10-second video and download both clips. 7. Combine both clips using CapCut or another video editor to create your final romantic reel.
