### Photo Prompt – ARYA 2 Cinematic Black & White Poster


Create a premium, ultra-realistic cinematic **4:5 vertical movie-poster photograph** inspired by the uploaded reference image. Recreate the same dramatic monochrome composition, stylish fashion photography, emotional double-exposure effect, and iconic ARYA 2 title design.


**Male Subject:** Show a stylish young Indian man standing confidently on the right side of the frame, wearing a textured white long-sleeve polo shirt, black cargo pants, white high-top sneakers, and dark sunglasses. His curly, voluminous hairstyle and natural facial features should look realistic. He should have a relaxed, charming smile, one hand adjusting his sunglasses and the other hand inside his trouser pocket. Pose him with his legs casually crossed at the ankles.


**Double-Exposure Background:** On the left and center, create a large, soft, semi-transparent monochrome portrait of a beautiful young Indian woman looking upward with a gentle, thoughtful expression. Preserve natural facial details, expressive eyes, long flowing dark hair, and a small black bindi. Behind her portrait, include a large dark silhouette of a curly-haired man adjusting his sunglasses, blending artistically into the background.


**Background & Lighting:** Use a minimalist studio environment with a soft smoky grey backdrop, subtle atmospheric haze, diffused cinematic lighting, gentle highlights, realistic floor shadows, and a polished black-and-white photographic finish. Keep the subjects sharply defined while the double-exposure portraits blend smoothly into the background.


**Title Design:** Place the title “ARYA 2” prominently in the lower-left area. Use bold, aggressive black brush lettering for “ARYA,” a vivid red brushstroke heart motif integrated around the letter A, and a large red painted number “2.” Add a tiny black heart detail inside the red heart. The lettering should look hand-painted, dynamic, distressed, and cinematic.


**Color Palette:** Predominantly black, white, and smoky grey, with selective bright crimson-red accents only in the heart and number 2.


**Image Quality:** Ultra-realistic photography, professional DSLR quality, detailed hair and clothing textures, realistic anatomy and hands, high contrast, soft film grain, premium Indian romantic-action movie-poster aesthetic, sharp focus, 4K detail, clean composition.


**Aspect Ratio:** Vertical 4:5.


**Negative Prompt:** Cartoon, anime, colorful background, blurry faces, distorted anatomy, extra fingers, malformed hands, duplicate subjects, unnatural poses, oversaturated colors, plastic skin, excessive retouching, misspelled title, extra text, watermarks, logos, low resolution.
